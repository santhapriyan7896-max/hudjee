The choices behind the app, and how it decides what you practise next.

How HudJee picks your next questionItem response theory, without the maths.

Every question in the bank carries a difficulty, and a measure of how sharply it separates students who know the idea from students who don’t. Both are fitted from real students’ answers. After each answer, HudJee updates its estimate of your level and serves the question that tells it the most, which is usually one right at the edge of what you can do.

Where the questions come fromJEE Main papers, worked solutions and a review queue.

The bank is built on JEE Main previous-year papers, each with a worked solution, alongside questions written and reviewed in HudJee’s own content studio. If three students report a problem with a question, it comes out of practice automatically until it’s fixed.

Phones and tabletsAndroid and iPhone, and laid out properly on an iPad.

HudJee lays itself out for the screen it’s on. Phones get the tab bar, tablets get a rail down the side, and a wide screen gets a full sidebar. In a practice session on a tablet, the question sits on top and the answers get their own pane along the bottom. Turn an iPad sideways and the app turns with it.
