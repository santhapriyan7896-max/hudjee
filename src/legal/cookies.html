In short

  • HudJee’s website doesn’t use cookies.
  • It saves one item in your browser so it remembers that you’ve registered.
  • There are no analytics, advertising or social-media trackers.

Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small files that a website asks your browser to keep, and that are sent back to the website each time you visit. Browser storage (often called “local storage”) is similar, but what’s stored there stays on your device and isn’t sent anywhere automatically.

Cookies we use

None. hudjee.com sets no cookies of its own, and it doesn’t load any advertising, analytics or social-media scripts that would set them. That’s why there’s no cookie banner: there’s nothing to accept or decline.

What we save in your browser

The site saves one item, and only after you register:

NameWhat it holdsWhyHow long
hudjee:pre-registered The email address you registered with. So that if you come back, the page shows you’re already on the list instead of asking you to register again. Until you click “Register someone else” or clear this site’s data.

This item stays on your device. The site never sends it to us or to anyone else.

Other services the page uses

  • Google Fonts. Your browser downloads the site’s typeface from Google’s servers, so Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request. Google says its Fonts service doesn’t set cookies. See Google Fonts and privacy.
  • Vercel hosts the site and delivers each page to your browser. It receives the visit details described in our privacy policy, and we don’t ask it to set any cookies.
  • The share button opens your phone’s or computer’s own share menu. We don’t see what you share or who you share it with.

How to control it

  • To remove the saved item, click “Register someone else” on the home page, or clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings.
  • In a private or incognito window, nothing is kept after you close it.
  • If your browser blocks storage, the site still works. It just won’t remember that you’ve registered.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add something that uses cookies, such as analytics, we’ll update this page first and ask for your permission where the law requires it.
